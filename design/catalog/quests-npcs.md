**Wolves of the Bamboo** — Spirit wolves prowl the Whispering Bamboo Forest to the west and trample the herb beds. Slay four of them and gather two Spirit Herbs (lingzhi, press E beside the glowing mushrooms). The sect will reward you.

**The Fox of the Peach Grove** — Well done. Now a greater trouble: a nine-tailed fox spirit has made its den in the Peach Grove, north-west of here, and bewitches travellers. Slay it.

**The Fallen Sword Immortal** — Beyond the Peach Grove, in the north-east court, a sword immortal who fell from grace stirs once more. Defeat the Fallen Sword Immortal and the sect may finally rest.

**The Dragon of the Mist Pool** — One last trial, champion. Deep in Mistveil Marsh, east of the southern road, a jiao dragon has woken in its pool. Barges vanish and the mist grows thick. Slay the Jiao Dragon, and bring me its pearl.

**Toads in the Ledger** — Marsh toads keep swallowing my supply barges in Mistveil Marsh, south of here. Thin out six of them and I will make it worth your while.

**Herbs for the Furnace** — My furnace is hungry and my herb baskets are empty. Bring me five Spirit Herbs (lingzhi, glowing mushrooms) and I will share what I brew.

**Trial of Champions** — Some beasts grow beyond their kind, wearing a golden aura and strange powers. Prove your mastery: slay two of these elite monsters.
